# MLS-SND work claim — es-GT rhotics wave 03

**Branch:** `snd/es-gt-rhotics-wave-03`  
**Base:** `feat/mls-snd-foundation`  
**Type:** research + drafting

## Claimed ID

- SND-ESGT-006 — Róticas

## Scope

Build a bounded rhotics entry with three evidence layers:

1. national/geolinguistic evidence from SND-SRC-0025 (Utgård 2010);
2. the Guatemala City Acevedo-Halvick 2005 study only as a secondary research lead until the primary text is checked;
3. recent Spanish–K’ichee’ bilingual sociophonetic evidence from Baird 2025, explicitly limited to that population.

## Limits

- no claim that assibilated rhotics “come from” K’ichee’;
- no generalization from bilingual data to all Guatemalan Spanish;
- no silent modernization of source-specific rhotic symbols;
- no claim of one national rhotic realization;
- no global Source Registry ID allocation from this worker branch.
