# 🇯🇵 Japanese writing/pronunciation — Source map wave 03

**Scope:** JA-008 kana/pronunciation; JA-009 kanji/readings/pronunciation.  
**Policy:** local source IDs remain branch-local until source-integration canonicalization.

## JA-W3-S01 — 現代仮名遣い / Modern Kana Usage

- **Institution:** Government of Japan / Agency for Cultural Affairs.
- **Instrument:** Cabinet Notice No. 1 (1986), amended in 2010.
- **Current institutional page:** https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/gendaikana/index.html
- **Scope:** modern kana orthography for general social use.
- **Key locators:** 前書き points 1–7; 本文 第1; 本文 第2.
- **Important evidence:**
  - spelling follows modern phonology as a principle while preserving orthographic convention through stated exceptions;
  - it does not aim to decide between variable pronunciations;
  - particles を/は/へ and several long-vowel spellings are convention-governed;
  - it does not necessarily correspond to rules for writing Japanese in roman letters.
- **Limit:** explicitly excludes foreign words/foreign sounds from this instrument.
- **Tier candidate:** A.
- **Status:** VERIFIED_FOR_WAVE.

## JA-W3-S02 — 外来語の表記 / Orthography of Loanwords

- **Institution:** Government of Japan / Agency for Cultural Affairs.
- **Instrument:** Cabinet Notice No. 2 (1991).
- **Current institutional page:** https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/gairai/index.html
- **Scope:** writing loanwords and foreign names with katakana.
- **Key locators:** 前書き; 留意事項その1; 仮名と符号の表; 留意事項その2.
- **Important evidence:**
  - this policy concerns representing loanwords/foreign names in katakana;
  - it does not attempt to choose one form when word forms vary;
  - conventional spellings may prevail;
  - some kana are used when trying to represent original pronunciation/spelling more closely;
  - special sounds outside the listed tables are not exhaustively regulated.
- **Limit:** orthographic policy, not a phonetic grammar of Japanese.
- **Tier candidate:** A.
- **Status:** VERIFIED_FOR_WAVE.

## JA-W3-S03 — 常用漢字表 / Jōyō Kanji Table (2010)

- **Institution:** Government of Japan / Agency for Cultural Affairs.
- **Instrument:** Cabinet Notice No. 2, 30 November 2010.
- **Current institutional page:** https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/kanji/
- **Scope:** guide to kanji use for writing modern Japanese in general social life.
- **Key locators:** 内閣告示; 前書き; 表の見方及び使い方; 本表/付表.
- **Important evidence:**
  - current table is a usage guide, not an exhaustive dictionary;
  - it includes characters, forms, 音訓 and examples;
  - proper names are generally outside scope except stated prefectural-name exceptions;
  - current table contains 2,136 characters.
- **Tier candidate:** A.
- **Status:** VERIFIED_FOR_WAVE.

## JA-W3-S04 — 常用漢字表の音訓索引

- **Institution:** Agency for Cultural Affairs.
- **URL:** https://www.bunka.go.jp/kokugo_nihongo/sisaku/joho/joho/kijun/naikaku/kanji/joyokanjisakuin/index.html
- **Scope:** searchable index of 音 and 訓 in the 2010 Jōyō Kanji Table.
- **Key locators:** page intro; 音・訓検索; individual character rows.
- **Important evidence:**
  - 音 searches use katakana;
  - 訓 searches use hiragana;
  - a single character can have multiple listed 音/訓 with different lexical examples.
- **Example used for audit:** 下 has multiple listed 音/訓 and lexical examples.
- **Tier candidate:** A.
- **Status:** VERIFIED_FOR_WAVE.

## Editorial boundary

None of these sources is used to claim that:
- kana spelling uniquely determines narrow phonetic realization;
- kanji has one intrinsic pronunciation;
- Jōyō readings exhaust personal/place-name readings;
- orthographic conventions encode Tokyo pitch accent;
- kana/kanji replace IPA when MLS-SND needs phonetic precision.
