## Entry verdicts

### IT-001 — PASS_WITH_SCOPE_WARNING

The distinction among Standard Italian, regional standards/varieties and “mainstream Italian” remains necessary. The source speaker is not treated as “all Italian”.

### IT-004 — PASS_SCOPE

Gemination is treated as a phonetic/phonological contrast with source-bounded evidence, not as a synonym for double spelling.

### IT-005 — PASS

The canonical version preserves the critical distinctions:
- free stress ≠ random stress;
- penultimate preference ≠ categorical rule;
- lexical stress ≠ vowel length;
- phonetic duration ≠ phonological quantity;
- orthographic accent ≠ complete phonological transcription.

SND-SRC-0074/0075 are canonical and the claims were re-audited after integration.

### IT-008 — PASS

Orthography is treated as the primary written layer, not as phonetic transcription. DOP is used as an orthoepic/lexicographic source rather than a complete regional phonetic grammar.

### IT-009 — PASS_WITH_SCOPE_WARNING

SND-SRC-0073 supports comparison of Standard Italian with Florence, Milan and Rome varieties and the documented regional divergence in stressed mid-vowel distribution.

FI/MI/RI remain documented comparators, not an exhaustive taxonomy of Italy.
